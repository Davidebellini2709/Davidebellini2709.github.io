Fishing Season

Step into the Alaskan wilderness and help Gunnar the bear catch his salmons in the river!

Instructions:

1- Use the arrow keys to move Gunnar the bear left and right. 2- Catch the salmons that are jumping out of the river pressing the space bar 3- Be careful not to eat the green ones! they are rotten and they are gonna make you loose one of your 3 lifes! 4- Catch 100 salmons to win the game! Have fun and enjoy the game! Made by Davide Bellini with the help of Cloude AI.
