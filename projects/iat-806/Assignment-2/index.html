assignment 2 — Pizza Rain, prove your Italianity

Only real Italian pizza will be allowed in this mouth. Don't you dare to eat the one with pineapple, or you will be remigrated to the U.S.A!

Instructions:

Click or press the space bar to start. Pizzas fall from the sky: open your mouth wide to eat margherita, salamino, rucola and bianca. The game uses your face with the ml5.js faceMesh model, so you have to move your whole head to catch them. Eat 15 pizzas to become Real Italian Certified, but if you eat 3 pizzas with pineapple you lose. Press M to play with the mouse instead of the camera. Code on GitHub
