Prototype

An interactive visual for electronic music. Inspired by the misty, mysterious atmosphere of the Pacific Northwest.

Instructions:

Press PLAY. The camera walks through an old-growth forest of cedars, firs, hemlocks and mossy maples. When the track is quiet the forest is still and foggy; as it grows more intense the walk becomes a run, the fog lifts and amber light cuts through the trees. Only in the most intense moments does each kick send a pulse of light through the forest, and a drop floods it with light. The mids fill the light with floating dust, and the highs make the rain heavier when it rains.

Everything else happens by itself: the day goes from blue hour to dawn, an overcast day, sunset and night; rain, fog and haze come and go; every so often the forest opens onto a two-lane road with the Misty Cove Diner, the Cedar Hollow auto body shop or the Snake Creek General Store. Deer, bears, crows and abandoned cars appear like apparitions.

Head tracking: press C and allow the camera. Hold your head still for a second while it calibrates, then turn or tilt your head to look around the forest (up to about 35° to the sides and 25° up and down). Press C again to switch it off. Face tracking runs in the browser with MediaPipe, and no image leaves your computer.

Extra features: double-click the canvas (or press F while the pointer is over it) for fullscreen, M to use the microphone, S for a simulated track, H to show the sound readings, +/- to adjust sensitivity, and space to pause. You can also drop any audio file onto the canvas.
