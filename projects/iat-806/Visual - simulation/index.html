Overgrown

An interactive visual for electronic music. Inspired by the misty, mysterious atmosphere of the Pacific Northwest.

Instructions:

Press PLAY. The track starts and the forest reacts to it: the kick pulses the orange glow behind the clouds, the bass thickens the fog and the undergrowth, the mids stir the orange particles in the air, the highs bring more rain and make the outline of the trees flare, and the overall energy speeds up the walk and brings more apparitions. Extra features: double-click the canvas (or press F while the pointer is over it) for fullscreen, M to use the microphone, S for a simulated beat, H to show the sound readings, +/- to adjust sensitivity, and space to pause. You can also drop any audio file onto the canvas.
